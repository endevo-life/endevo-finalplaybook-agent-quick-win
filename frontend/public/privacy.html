Privacy Policy

ENDevo Inc. · Last updated: August 9, 2026 · Contact: hello@endevo.life

My Final Playbook (the "Service") is operated by ENDevo Inc. ("ENDevo", "we"). This policy describes what we collect, why, and what we never do with it. It supplements the ENDevo master privacy policy; where the two differ for this app, this page controls.

What we collect

  • Your email address — the only identifier we ask for. Sign-in is a one-time code sent to your email; there is no password.
  • Your planning data — assessment answers, your generated action plan, progress you check off, details you type into plan fields, the personalized narrative, and your chat history with the guide. Stored so you can pick up where you left off, on any device.
  • Subscription status — your plan tier and a Stripe customer reference. Your card details never touch our systems; payment happens entirely on Stripe's hosted checkout.
  • Feedback you send — the message, an optional rating, and the page you sent it from.
  • Basic service events — e.g. that a signup or upgrade happened, used for operating the Service. Recorded server-side; we do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the app.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage holds your session token and in-progress work so a refresh doesn't sign you out or lose your place. Signing out clears the session.

How AI is used

Paid features (the personalized narrative and follow-up chat, plus a small free chat allowance) send your plan content and first name to a large-language-model provider (AWS Bedrock, or Anthropic) to generate a response. The AI is only permitted to rephrase and personalize content written by people — it is not used to invent advice. We do not use your data to train AI models.

Email

We email you sign-in codes. Internal operator notifications (e.g. "a new member signed up") contain identity information only — your answers, plan, and narrative are never included in any outbound email.

What we never do

  • Sell your data, or share it with advertisers.
  • Put your planning answers, plan, or narrative into email.
  • Store your card number — that is Stripe's, and only Stripe's.

Service providers

We use Amazon Web Services (hosting, database, email delivery, and Bedrock AI), Stripe (payments), and Anthropic (AI, where configured). Each receives only what it needs to perform its function.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and all associated data, email hello@endevo.life from your account address and we will remove it. (An in-app "Delete my account" control is planned.)

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it at any time via the contact above. If you are in a jurisdiction with specific privacy rights (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), we honor requests consistent with that law.

Children

The Service is intended for adults planning their own affairs and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note the new date above and, for significant changes, notify you by email.
